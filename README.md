A launcher built around a rotary dial, made to help you pick up your phone, do what you came for and put it down again.

## Introduction
Axiris is a launcher with a single idea at its core: a **dial**, like the ones on old phones, sitting at the bottom of the screen. You spin it to a letter, and the apps starting with that letter show up. Above it, a big clock and your widgets. That's it, and that's the point: there's no endless grid of icons to scroll through while you forget why you unlocked the phone.

It's not a minimal launcher though, at least not in the "black screen and grey text" sense. I wanted something calm but beautiful, with animations that feel physical, colors taken from your wallpaper and a lot of small details to discover. Being mindful doesn't mean being boring 😄

The code of the app is private, but this repository is where everything else lives: the **wiki**, the **issues**, the **translations** and the **documentation for developers** who want to add their app to the Axiris search.

## Features
- A rotary dial to reach any app by its letter, with haptic ticks, flings, a quick scroll strip and a lot of pointer and arc styles
- Favorites, folders, hidden apps and apps excluded from the launcher but still searchable
- A rich clock (with a word clock option) and a free widget area, rotation included
- Fovea, a sheet full of information: weather, air quality, pollen, tides, moon, calendar, network, RSS feeds and more
- A search that does much more than finding apps: contacts with call and message actions, calendar events, system settings, calculations, unit and currency conversions, alarms, timers, websites and results from other apps
- Themes: presets, your own themes, and `.axtheme` files to share them with anyone
- Custom pointers and arcs, made with a visual editor: symbols, your own pictures, live data, colors and motions
- Blur and colors adapted to your wallpaper, Material 3 Expressive and Monet everywhere
- Notification dots in four styles, and the notifications of an app one swipe away
- Kids mode, which makes the launcher almost unusable for a kid (it's a feature, I promise)
- Backup and restore of every setting
- A bunch of secret dials. No spoilers here, go find them 👀
- No ads, no tracking, and the permissions that need your consent (contacts, calendar, location) are asked only when you turn on the feature that uses them
